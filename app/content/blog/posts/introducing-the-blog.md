Welcome to the blog section of my site.

## What to expect

This is a place for engineering notes, technical deep dives, and lessons from building software. I'll share practical ideas and the trade-offs behind them.

## A simple publishing workflow

The blog keeps content alongside the site's code:

1. Write an article in plain Markdown.
2. Add its title, description, and publication date to a small JSON file.
3. Review the changes, run the tests, and deploy.

Articles support headings, lists, links, and code blocks. Markdown is converted to HTML on the server and sanitized before rendering.

There are no blog view counters or local-storage tracking. You can browse the articles without a blog consent banner.
